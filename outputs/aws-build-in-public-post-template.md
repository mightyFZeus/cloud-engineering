[One security, reliability, networking, cost, or architecture tradeoff in plain language.]

[Repository/diagram/demo link]

## Optional carousel or image captions

1. The outcome: what now works.
2. The architecture: request/event flow and trust boundaries.
3. The problem: observed symptom and impact.
4. The evidence: logs, metrics, events, or test result.
5. The fix: decision and tradeoff.
6. The next milestone.

## Pre-publish safety and quality check

- [ ] No AWS account IDs, access keys, session tokens, secrets, private URLs, personal email addresses, or unnecessary IP addresses are visible.
- [ ] Screenshots have been cropped/redacted and browser tabs/terminal history checked.
- [ ] Terraform state, environment variables, logs, and Git history contain no secrets.
- [ ] The post distinguishes tutorial practice from production experience.
- [ ] Every claimed result has a test, command, screenshot, metric, or working link behind it.
- [ ] The post describes an outcome or insight rather than only naming technologies.
- [ ] The tone sounds like me and avoids exaggerated claims such as “mastered AWS.”
